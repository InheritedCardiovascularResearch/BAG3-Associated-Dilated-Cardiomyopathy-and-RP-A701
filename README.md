# BAG3-Associated Dilated Cardiomyopathy and RP-A701

Research and educational resources on BAG3-associated dilated cardiomyopathy, BAG3 biology, RP-A701, and cardiovascular gene therapy development.

## Overview

BAG3-associated dilated cardiomyopathy (BAG3-DCM) is a genetic form of dilated cardiomyopathy associated with variants in the **BAG3** gene. The condition is relevant to research into inherited cardiovascular disease and potential gene replacement strategies.

Rocket Pharmaceuticals is developing **RP-A701**, an investigational gene therapy program for BAG3-associated dilated cardiomyopathy. This repository organizes research materials, scientific publications, clinical development information, and educational resources related to the disease and the program.

The goal is to make information about BAG3-DCM and RP-A701 easier to locate, review, and understand through a structured collection of research and educational references.

## Source and Evidence Standards

To maintain the accuracy and usefulness of this repository:

1. **Prioritize primary sources.** Use scientific publications, clinical trial records, company announcements, and regulatory documents.
2. **Preserve publication dates.** Record when a source was published and when any clinical update was reported.
3. **Distinguish evidence types.** Separate preclinical research, clinical trial plans, preliminary findings, and confirmed outcomes.
4. **Avoid unsupported conclusions.** Do not infer efficacy, safety, regulatory approval, or development milestones from indirect evidence.
5. **Keep disease targets distinct.** Do not conflate BAG3-associated dilated cardiomyopathy with PKP2-associated arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy or Danon disease.
6. **Update responsibly.** When new information becomes available, add the original source and clarify what has changed.
